Who we are

VEGOS V2 is a roleplay crew inside Roblox's Emergency Hamburg — built around one radio channel, one crest, and a founder who never really signed off.

The original VEGOS ran its shifts, built its name around Hamburg's streets, and then went quiet. Two years later the channel is open again, rebuilt from the ground up by founder Nprobie as VEGOS V2 — same crest, sharper crew.

Whether you were on the roster the first time around or you're checking in for the first time, dispatch is live and the scene log is open below.
